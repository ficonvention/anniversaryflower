Wedding and party flowers styled through a prior meeting with our florist — romantic, classic and beautifully finished.

The Anniversary Party & Flower team has designed weddings, first birthdays, family banquets and corporate events at Sebitseom Floating Island and Marina Park. With rose petals and candles, we create romantic party setups with an exotic atmosphere.

Having planned first-birthday celebrations for many celebrity families, Anniversary Flower — led by a director with a PhD in design, together with experienced designers and a veteran flower team — creates an unforgettable first birthday for both baby and parents.

For 20 years Anniversary Flower has styled major embassy events — Bastille Day, the U.S. Embassy's Independence Day, the national days of India, Sweden and Indonesia — as well as state-visit dinners and launch events for luxury brands, Hyundai and Samsung. With exquisite colour, flawless finish and only the freshest premium flowers, we elevate every banquet.
